Senior Backend Engineer — Platform Team

We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to help scale our real-time data
platform. You'll own services that process high-volume event streams and work
closely with product teams to turn ambiguous requirements into reliable,
well-tested systems.

Responsibilities:
- Design and operate distributed, event-driven services handling millions of
  events per day
- Improve system observability (metrics, tracing, logging) and drive down
  incident detection and resolution time
- Lead architecture reviews and mentor mid-level engineers
- Own reliability for critical services, including on-call rotations and
  incident response
- Partner with infrastructure teams to improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment
  speed

Requirements:
- 5+ years of professional backend engineering experience
- Strong experience with Go, Java, or Python in production systems
- Hands-on experience with Kafka or similar event streaming platforms
- Experience with PostgreSQL or another relational database at scale
- Familiarity with Kubernetes, Docker, and cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP)
- Experience with observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry,
  or similar)
- Track record of mentoring engineers and leading technical initiatives

Nice to have:
- Experience with microservices migrations from a monolithic architecture
- Experience with CI/CD tooling such as GitHub Actions or Jenkins
- Background working in regulated or compliance-heavy environments
